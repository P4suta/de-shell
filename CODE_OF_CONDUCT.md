# Code of conduct

de-shell welcomes participation across experience levels, identities, and
backgrounds. Be respectful, specific, and patient. Critique ideas and observable
behavior, not people. Assume good intent while still taking reported harm
seriously.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure
of private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project
spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed
to protect the community.

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainer through the contact
options on [@P4suta's GitHub profile](https://github.com/P4suta). For threats,
abuse, or platform-policy violations, also use GitHub's report-content controls.
Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the moderation
path. Reports are handled as privately and impartially as the available channel
allows, with conflicts of interest disclosed where relevant.
